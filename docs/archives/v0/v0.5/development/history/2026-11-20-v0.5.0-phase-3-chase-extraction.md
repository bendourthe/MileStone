# v0.5.0 Phase 3 | Chase Card and Benefit Extraction

- **Date**: 2026-11-20
- **Plan**: [v0.5.0 Chase Personal and Business Benefits](../../plans/v0.5.0-chase-benefits.md), Phase 3 (T007-T009)
- **Branch**: `feat/v0.5.0-chase-benefits`, on top of the Phase 2 commit `9c2832f`
- **Decision**: [ADR-0009](../../../../../decisions/0009-chase-benefits.md)

## Summary

MileStone can now read Chase cards and benefits through every signed-in Chase connection. The read follows the owner's own path to the Benefits page, lists the card-selector sheet, switches cards through it, and stores each card's rows and spend trackers under its local card identity. Amex reading is unchanged.

## Source evidence used

- Owner direction (2026-11-20): select a card, then More, then Benefits.
- Privacy-filtered structure of the sanitized captures (tag names, digit-free class tokens, attribute names, allowlisted test ids, text shapes with digits as 9 and only generic words kept): the Benefits root `[data-testid=benefits-hub-page]`, the card-selector button and its `[data-testid=card-selector-sheet]` dialog with one unnamed radio per `li[data-selected]`, benefit groups `ul[data-testid^=nonFeaturedBenefits-]` with `li.nonFeaturedListItem-listItem` rows (title, optional tag such as activated or redeemed, optional value such as an amount and period), and `mds-progress-bar` spend trackers.
- The More menu and the dashboard's card entries are design-system components whose contents are not in the captures; navigation therefore matches fixed interface labels ("More", "Benefits") and a card ending, inside shadow roots, and stops when a control is missing.

## Work

- **T007 (adapter)**:
  - `BenefitsSpec` gains an `adapter` (AMEX default, CHASE) with per-adapter selector keys; `CHASE_BENEFITS` is attached to the Chase program.
  - `extract.js` Chase mode: re-entrant per page; navigation note survives single-page route changes; inventory read twice before posting; switch confirmed by content change or by the sheet; read after 2 s of stable structure with no busy marker; fixed unreadable reasons (`navigation` with its step, `timeout`, `switch`, `noSuchCard`, `structure`); a sign-in form stops the read.
  - `ChaseBenefitsSource` reads each signed-in connection in turn under one shared budget, each in its own profile; a card already read through an earlier connection (same binding hash) is skipped, including the card the page shows first.
  - `BindingHasher`: HMAC-SHA256 under a per-install key in the no-backup folder; raw control ids stay in memory for the read only.
  - `AdapterBenefitsSource` routes reads by adapter; `SyncManager.applyBenefits` applies connection inventories, then stores benefits through `cardKeyForBinding`.
- **T008 (normalization)**: `ChaseBenefitsReader` validates every message field and cap, and maps rows to listed benefits (amount and period from the value text, enrollment and completion from the tag) and trackers to spend progress (earned only from stated dollar amounts or a bar whose maximum is the target). Nothing is invented; unparseable values stay null.

## Independent Review

A read-only security and privacy review of the Chase mode, source, reader, hasher and protocol found 2 blockers, 3 should-fix items and 2 nits; it confirmed no path that can move money, leak raw control ids, or bypass nonce and page checks. All were fixed:

- Blocker: the card-entry press could match a payment or transfer control showing an ending. It now takes the first entry in page order, never inside a form or dialog, and never one whose label names an action or a deposit account.
- Blocker: an identity-check page offering "Text ...1234" could be pressed. The read now stops as signed out whenever a code, phone or number field or a radio outside the card-selector sheet is visible.
- Should-fix: the selector button is pressed at most once per 1.5 s; a connection stops after 8 page loads; each navigation step waits for a visible change before looking for its next control.
- Nits: tracker text comes only from the tracker's own link or parent with endings removed; a card name never keeps its ending.

Regression tests: `aPaymentControlShowingAnEndingIsNeverPressed` and `anIdentityCheckStopsTheReadWithoutChoosingADeliveryOption` pass (6 of 6 device tests), and removing both guards makes exactly those two fail; the script was restored byte-identical.

## CI Impact

None to the pipeline. New unit tests run in the existing `testDebugUnitTest` step; the private capture test skips in CI (no captures there) and is required locally with `MILESTONE_REQUIRE_CHASE_PRIVATE_CAPTURES=1`. The new device test runs in the existing connected-test step of the full and platform profiles.

## Plan delta

- **Evidence**: the card entry, More and Benefits controls are unobserved in the captures; the selector's radio ids are masked in them; Chase timing is still an estimate.
