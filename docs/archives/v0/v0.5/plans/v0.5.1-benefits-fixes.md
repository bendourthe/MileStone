# Plan | v0.5.1 Benefits Fixes After Owner Testing

**Project**: MileStone
**Version**: v0.5.1
**Slug**: benefits-fixes
**Plan Type**: Bug-fix campaign
**Created**: 2026-11-21
**Status**: released
**Goal**: Fix what the owner found on the phone after v0.5.0 (Chase progress and credits, the finished Hilton free night tracker, card names and artwork, tab and page navigation), verify each change on the phone, and release v0.5.1 before v0.6.

## Overview

The owner tested v0.5.0 on the phone on 2026-11-21 and reported the defects below. The owner chose a v0.5.1 patch before v0.6 and approved each fix after seeing it on the phone. This plan records that work after the fact: every task is already implemented on branch `fix/v0.5-trackers-navigation` and verified by the local gate, emulator tests, private captures, and the owner's phone. It was not run through `/implement`, so it has no native run record; the owner's "release" reply on 2026-11-21 authorizes this release.

Owner data never defines coverage: card names, endings, and counts on the phone are evidence, not rules, except the one catalog decision in Phase 3, which the owner directed.

## Phase 1 | Chase Progress and Credits

- [x] T001 Read a Chase spend tracker's progress from its gauge's accessible text ("$X received of $Y") and title it from the tile heading (`app/src/main/assets/extract.js`)
- [x] T002 Join a Chase row's two-part value with " - " and group listed credits under Credits (`app/src/main/java/com/bendourthe/milestone/ui/benefits/BenefitsPresentation.kt`)
- [x] T003 Open each Chase credit's own page, read its "$X/$Y" gauge, and go back, at most 8 per card (`app/src/main/java/com/bendourthe/milestone/sync/extract/ChaseBenefitsReader.kt`)
- [x] T004 Log credit pages planned and used amounts read per Chase card (`app/src/main/java/com/bendourthe/milestone/sync/webview/ChaseBenefitsSource.kt`)

## Phase 2 | Amex Finished Trackers and Lists

- [x] T005 Keep a finished "Congratulations!" tracker and name it from the listed benefit, the card's saved list, or its own "earned a ..." words when the card is saved (`app/src/main/java/com/bendourthe/milestone/sync/SyncManager.kt`)
- [x] T006 At the Rewards readiness cap, follow the view-all link when trackers are on screen, so a page that never reaches its bottom still refreshes its list (`app/src/main/assets/extract.js`)

## Phase 3 | Card Names and Artwork

- [x] T007 Show a card the catalog knows by its catalog product name, shortened to the product, and any other card by the issuer's name cleaned (`app/src/main/java/com/bendourthe/milestone/domain/CardNames.kt`)

## Not In Scope

- The best card per store or category view (earn rates from the Amex Points page and later Chase), which the owner placed in its own plan after v0.5.1.
- Keeping a Chase credit's last used amount when its page is not read again: Chase gives no period end for these credits, so a kept amount could stay wrong after the year changes.
- The on-phone Rename card feature, built and then removed at the owner's direction in favor of catalog names.
