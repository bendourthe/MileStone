# Plan | Chase Personal and Business Benefits

**Project**: MileStone
**Version**: v0.5.0
**Slug**: chase-benefits
**Plan Type**: Feature/Enhancement
**Created**: 2026-11-14
**Status**: draft
**Goal**: Deliver reachable Chase personal/business credit cards, welcome information, credits and benefits through separate Benefits tiles and independent or linked sign-ins, without duplicate cards or loss of Amex data/sign-ins, then verify, integrate, release and close.

## Overview

The owner's business login can see both personal and business cards; a personal login may see only personal cards. Other users need independent logins. Connections must therefore be separate from the two visible tiles; linked access populates both. Read welcome tracking where exposed and retain manual setup otherwise. Keep existing Chase Rewards behavior and the accepted v0.4 presentation.

The confirmed release order is v0.4.0, v0.5.0 then v0.6.0. Existing v0.1/v0.2/v0.3 and v0.4 obligations remain. This draft does not itself grant remote authority. After decisions and native admission, /implement follows one local commit per phase, one final publication to develop, green CI result, /update release, archive and eligible cleanup. The active native goal cannot edit its objective, so docs/todos.md retains scope without falsely completing it.

## Owner Generalization Requirement

Owner clarification, 2026-11-16: a connected Chase account must dynamically expose every accessible personal and business card and its source-provided rewards, welcome bonus, credits, trackers and listed benefits, including products and account arrangements absent from the owner captures. The same generalization requirement applies to Amex. Captures establish adapter structure and test regressions; owner card names, endings, counts, benefit titles and expected totals must never define a production inventory or completion condition. No owner-account allowlist or card-order assumption is permitted.

Verification must include synthetic accounts with zero, one and more cards than the captured baseline; unfamiliar product names; reordered, newly added and removed cards; lazy or paginated inventories; separate and linked profiles; and equal-ending distinct cards. Exercise inventory and item counts across transport safety boundaries. A safety cap, unsupported section or read deadline must preserve existing data and report explicit incomplete coverage instead of silently omitting items or claiming all-card completion. A novel product may use fallback artwork without losing extraction or navigation. These are explicit cases for the existing identity, discovery, completeness and final-acceptance tasks; no task is checked by this clarification.

The [generalization verification matrix](../development/planning/account-generalization-matrix-20261116.json) maps twelve required account variations to existing tasks. Its cases are not executed and do not establish source GO.
