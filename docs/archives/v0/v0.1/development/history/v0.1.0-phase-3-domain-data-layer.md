## Work Done

- `domain/Models.kt`: `Program`, `ProgramCategory`, `SyncCapability` (`Automatic(ExtractionSpec)` or `ManualOnly(reason)`), `ExtractionSpec.Selector`, `FailureReason`, `SyncResult`, and the account and dashboard models; `domain/Repositories.kt` holds the `CatalogRepository`, `AccountRepository`, and `SettingsRepository` interfaces; `domain/CatalogSearch.kt` filters by name and category.
- `data/catalog/StaticCatalogRepository.kt`: 14 programs. Amex, Chase, Capital One, Alaska, Aeroplan, Hyatt, and Hilton are `Automatic` with placeholder `TODO-extractor` selectors marked `construction-debt:` for Phases 7 to 9; the other 7 are `ManualOnly` with a user-facing reason. Hosts and URLs come from `docs/releases/v0/v0.1/development/v0.1.0-site-feasibility.md`. Popular: Amex, Chase, Capital One, Delta, United, Marriott.
- `data/db/`: `LoyaltyAccountEntity` (`loyalty_accounts`) and `BalanceSnapshotEntity` (balance history), `LoyaltyDao`, and `MileStoneDatabase` version 1 with the schema exported to `app/schemas/com.bendourthe.milestone.data.db.MileStoneDatabase/1.json`; `data/RoomAccountRepository.kt` implements `AccountRepository`.
- `data/settings/DataStoreSettingsRepository.kt`: the sort mode (Alphabetical or Custom) in Preferences DataStore.
- `domain/usecase/`: `ParseBalanceInput` (manual entry), `SetManualBalance`, and `ObserveDashboard` (Alphabetical and Custom sorting).
- `AppContainer.kt` wired into `MileStoneApplication.onCreate`; it owns the database, catalog, repositories, session store, and `SyncManager`.
