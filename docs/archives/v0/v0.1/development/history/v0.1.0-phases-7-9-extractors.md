The three extractor phases ran together with the sign-in and sync work they depended on, across several device sessions, so this one record covers them. Tasks T031 to T039 are complete.

## Outcome

Every program with a tested sign-in reads its balance from its own site through a fixture-verified selector in `data/catalog/StaticCatalogRepository.kt`: Amex, Chase, and Capital One (Phase 7); Delta, United, Alaska, and Aeroplan (Phase 8); Marriott, Hyatt, and Hilton (Phase 9). Citi, Bilt, American Airlines, and British Airways are listed in Add program but cannot be added yet, because sign-in has not been tested yet (known gaps G01 to G04).

## Work Done

- Selectors: Amex and Chase use XPath (Chase reads the `mds-list-item` `label` attribute, so `extract.js` also observes attribute changes); Alaska reads `.loyalty-info-row .available-points .display-md`; Aeroplan reads the signed-in account menu's points label; Delta reads the header miles on the signed-in home page (`DELTA_XPATH`); United reads the number under the MILES heading (`UNITED_CSS`); Marriott reads the account panel, opening it once when closed (`MARRIOTT_CSS`, `revealSelector`). Capital One Rewards is cash in whole cents (`BalanceUnit.CASH_CENTS`).
- Fixtures: one synthetic page per selector under `app/src/test/resources/fixtures/` with a fake balance and decoy values; private captures stay in the gitignored `fixtures-private/`. `ExtractorFixtureTest` runs each selector against both and mirrors `document.evaluate` through Jsoup `W3CDom` and `javax.xml.xpath`.
- Capture build: labeled "MileStone Capture", keeps the sign-in sheet open after a failed read, and confirms each capture with a toast.
- Page script: searches open shadow roots and same-origin frames, reports whether an entry field is visible (never values), waits out loading text and a zero shown while an account loads, and counts only a visible error that appeared after typing as a refused password.
- Pulled forward from Phase 10: the manual data layer was removed (Room schema 2 migrates from 1), per ADR-0003.
