## Change

AuroraBackground retains its original colors, glow positions, radii, minute-long motion and animation-off behavior. Each composition owns one reusable high-precision raster with a longest side of at most 256 pixels. The original gradients are combined into that image at the current phase, then drawn across the background once. Stable phases reuse the image; raster dimension changes replace it. Query, navigation, issuer, storage and sign-in code are unchanged.
