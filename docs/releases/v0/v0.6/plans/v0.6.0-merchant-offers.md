**Goal**: Discover and maintain dynamic merchant offers on supported added cards and automatically add verified eligible offers by default while honoring card/campaign restrictions, confirming outcomes and preserving sign-ins, then verify, integrate, release and close.

## Overview

The owner explicitly chose automatic adding ON by default for eligible offers. Discovery and issuer-side activation are separate stages; this is not replaced with opt-in. Initial issuers are Amex and Chase personal/business cards using v0.5 identity. New/changed/expired offers are ordinary lifecycle events. Show additions, pending reasons and stop controls. Public Chase FAQ confirms personal/business primary cardmember or authorizing-officer access. The owner requires adding a matching offer on every eligible card, including multiple cards for the same merchant and value. Do not deduplicate across cards or impose a preferred-card list. Apply a single-card restriction only when current issuer terms or verified account state explicitly require it; identical offer text alone proves no such restriction. Sources: [Chase FAQ](https://www.chase.com/personal/merchant-offers/faqs), [Amex Offers](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/benefits/offers/).

The confirmed release order is v0.4.0, v0.5.0 then v0.6.0. Existing v0.1/v0.2/v0.3 and v0.4 obligations remain. This draft does not itself grant remote authority. After decisions and native admission, /implement follows one local commit per phase, one final publication to develop, green CI result, /update release, archive and eligible cleanup. The active native goal cannot edit its objective, so docs/todos.md retains scope without falsely completing it.

## Owner Generalization Requirement

Owner clarification, 2026-11-16: production must discover current merchant offers for each connected user and automatically add every independently eligible offer on every eligible card, for Amex and Chase personal/business accounts. Card products, merchants, offers, card counts and benefit/reward content may differ completely from the owner captures. No fixed owner inventory, merchant allowlist, captured offer count or captured display text may define production discovery or activation eligibility.

Verification must vary card and offer counts, unfamiliar products and merchants, pagination/lazy loading, new and removed offers, repeated merchants on different cards, changed ordering, overlapping connections and already-added states. The same eligible offer on two cards requires two separately confirmed additions. Limits and partial reads remain visibly incomplete; an activation is complete only after the issuer confirms its exact card and offer. Captures remain read-only, default-on production adding and pause controls remain required, and purchases or unrelated enrollments stay outside the action contract. These cases strengthen the existing discovery, action and final-acceptance tasks without marking them complete.

The [generalization verification matrix](../../../../archives/v0/v0.5/development/planning/account-generalization-matrix-20261116.json) maps twelve required account variations to existing tasks. Its cases are not executed and do not establish source GO.

The [initial issuer-rule evidence](../development/planning/issuer-offer-rules-20261117.json) records the public Chase FAQ check and earlier failed direct Amex-page reads. The [follow-up Amex clauses](../development/planning/amex-verified-public-clauses-20261117.json) now verify the relevant public enrollment, cardmember eligibility and unauthorized-automation restrictions through an ordinary HTTP200 fetch and contextual reading. Establish an issuer-authorized integration path and applicable cardmember/campaign scope before enabling live Amex offer interaction; owner consent, matching offer text and visible Add controls do not establish that issuer permission. These public artifacts do not establish account selectors, complete-list criteria or account source GO. Default-on adding on every independently eligible card remains the owner policy; this discovery does not replace it with manual-only adding, a preferred-card policy or cross-card merchant deduplication.
