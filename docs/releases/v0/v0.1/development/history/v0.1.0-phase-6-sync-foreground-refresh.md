**Date**: 2026-11-07

## Outcome

Opening MileStone refreshes every connected program in the background, using the program's saved sign-in, and the dashboard shows progress and errors per tile. Tasks T027 to T030 are complete. The phase commit follows the Phase 5 commit.

## Work Done

- `sync/WebViewHost.kt` port and `sync/webview/ActivityWebViewHost.kt`: a 1x1, invisible, non-focusable WebView attached to the activity for the duration of one fetch, then removed and destroyed.
- `sync/webview/HeadlessWebViewBalanceSource.kt`: reuses `LoginBridge` in headless mode, so one navigation policy and one listener serve both paths; any host outside the program's own hosts is blocked; a login page gives `NeedsReconnect`.
- `SyncManager` refresh rules: eligible programs are CONNECTED or ERROR, `Automatic`, with a session record and a registered host; at most 2 fetches at once, 30 s per fetch, at most one automatic refresh per program every 5 minutes, exponential backoff after errors capped at 6 hours, a repeated manual Refresh All within 10 s ignored, and cancellation that writes nothing.
- `sync/ForegroundSyncObserver.kt`: on start it registers the activity as host and refreshes all; on stop it cancels headless fetches and clears the host.
- Dashboard: Refresh All button with progress, pull-to-refresh, a spinner on syncing tiles, and an error chip ("Timed out", "Needs attention", "Could not read balance") that opens sign-in on tap and shows the full reason on long press.
- `AppContainer` and `MainActivity` wiring, replacing the Phase 1 placeholder screen.
