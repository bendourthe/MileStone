**Project**: MileStone
**Version**: v0.3.0
**Slug**: amex-benefits-extraction-fix
**Filename**: docs/releases/v0/v0.3/plans/v0.3.0-amex-benefits-extraction-fix.md
**Plan Type**: Other (bug-fix campaign)
**Created**: 2026-11-12
**Goal**: After an American Express refresh on the owner's phone, every card on the account (Platinum, Business Platinum, Hilton Surpass, Gold Delta SkyMiles) stores at least the credits, spend trackers, completed items, and listed benefits with enrollment status that the Amex site shows for that card, counted per category, within the existing 120 s benefits read.

## Overview

v0.2.0 reads each Amex card's benefits in a hidden WebView after the balance read (ADR-0008). On the owner's phone the read reached all 4 cards but stored a full set only for Business Platinum (5 credits, 6 spend trackers, 1 completed, 59 listed). Platinum and Hilton stored 1 completed item each, and Delta stored nothing. The v0.2 captures hold far more, so the selectors are not the main problem: the read posts from the wrong page or too early.

Three suspected causes come from structure-only counts of the private captures (tag, attribute, and link counts; no text read):

1. **The full list for Hilton and Delta lives on a page the read never opens.** Every card's Rewards page links to `/card-benefits/view-all`. Hilton and Delta have no activity page, and their Rewards page shows only 2 trackers and a 7-link carousel. ADR-0008 accepts only `/rewards` and `/card-benefits/activity`.
2. **The first card posts before the lower part of its page loads.** Platinum's Rewards capture contains the activity link, but Platinum is the card already shown when the page opens. Its 2 trackers stay steady for 2 s before the scroll-loaded section with the link renders, so the read posts from Rewards.
3. **Delta's in-place switch is read while its tracker area is empty.** The current switch rule waits until the tracker set "changed", and an emptied tracker area counts as changed, so the 6 s empty-page settle can fire before Delta's trackers arrive. Not yet confirmed live.

A fourth defect makes any partial read harmful: `applyCardBenefits` replaces a card's whole stored set, so a read that reaches only part of a card's pages erases what the other pages held.

The plan first makes the read explain itself (per-page and per-card counts in the diagnostics file) and captures the missing pages, then fixes timing and the switch rule within one run deadline, then adds the view-all list with section-scoped storage. Privacy rules stay as in v0.2: only named fields are read, identifiers are stripped in the page script, logs hold hosts, counts, and stop reasons only, and captures are inspected by structure only.

## Definition of Done

Must (the release ships with these):

1. **Per-card counts.** The capture record (T004) lists, for each card, the expected count per category (credits, spend trackers, completed, listed, enrolled), checked by the owner against the item counts the Amex site shows. On the owner's phone, on 3 separate Amex refreshes, the counts stored for Platinum, Business Platinum, and Delta's trackers are at least the expected counts, recorded in a table per refresh.
2. **No regression.** Business Platinum keeps at least 5 credits, 6 spend trackers, 1 completed, and 59 listed on every one of the 3 refreshes.
3. **Explainable.** The diagnostics file has one count-only line per page (kind, counts, wait, ready reason) and one per card (elapsed time, page count), plus the run's total time.
4. **Within budget.** Each of the 3 refreshes finishes its benefits read within `BENEFITS_TIMEOUT_MS` (120 s), with the total logged (owner decision 2026-11-12: completeness first, 2 minutes at most).
5. **Green.** The local gate, the instrumented suite on the emulator, the private-capture tests on the laptop, and the integration pull request's required check pass.

Should (Phase 3; v0.3.0 ships without it if it fails, owner decision 2026-11-12):

6. Hilton and Delta also store their view-all list with enrollment status, at least the expected listed and enrolled counts, on the same 3 refreshes; a failed view-all page keeps the card's previous list.
