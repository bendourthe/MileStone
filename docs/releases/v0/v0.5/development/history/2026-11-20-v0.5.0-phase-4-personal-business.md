# v0.5.0 Phase 4 | Personal and Business Benefits Experience

- **Date**: 2026-11-20

- **Branch**: `feat/v0.5.0-chase-benefits`, on top of the Phase 3 commit `6db5922`
- **Decision**: [ADR-0009](../../../../../decisions/0009-chase-benefits.md)

## Summary

The Benefits tab shows Chase as two tiles, Chase Personal and Chase Business, each listing its cards with their own stored ending. Each Chase page has a sign-ins section: mark every sign-in Personal or Business, add another isolated sign-in for that segment, or sign out an extra one. Search and reminders open the exact card by its local card id, so two cards with the same ending stay apart. Amex is unchanged.

## Work

- **T010 (tiles)**:
  - `BenefitsViewModel` groups Chase cards as `chase#personal` and `chase#business`; Personal always shows, Business once a card belongs to it; a card without a segment stays visible under Personal with "Needs Personal or Business".
  - Card tiles carry the stored ending (`lastDigits`) for their label and TalkBack text.
  - `ConnectionsViewModel` and `ConnectionsSection`: list sign-ins (the first marked "(balance)"), change a declaration, add a sign-in (refused with an explanation when the phone cannot isolate profiles, or at 4), sign out an extra one.
  - `LoginBridge` takes a `sessionKey` (default: the program) so an extra sign-in opens in its own profile without the saved password; `SignInViewModel.openConnection` records that sign-in's session when its account appears and never writes a balance, so Chase Rewards stays with the first sign-in and nothing is double-counted.
  - The dashboard's Benefits shortcut opens Chase Personal.
- **T011 (identity)**:
  - Search entries carry the card's stored ending (`cardDigits`) for matching and for the "Card ending" labels; their ids use the local card id.
  - A reminder's deep link accepts a local card id and opens that exact card (Amex links keep their path through the program page).
  - A missing card still returns to the list rather than opening another card.
