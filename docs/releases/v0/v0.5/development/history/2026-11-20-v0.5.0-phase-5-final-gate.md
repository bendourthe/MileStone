## Work

- **Cycle 1**:
  - The Chase read stopped after 8 page loads regardless of card count and reported the read as complete.
  - The page-load limit is now 8 plus 2 per listed card, and hitting it after the card list is a budget stop that keeps unread cards' stored benefits.
- **Cycle 2, from the independent convergence review**:
  - Over-cap cards (more than 30 groups, 150 rows or 20 trackers) are reported unreadable instead of stored partially.
  - The card page has a Show under menu that sets the Personal or Business override.
- **Cycle 2, from the independent adversarial review**:
  - The card list must be unchanged for 2 s before it is posted.
  - A complete list that shares no card with a sign-in's coverage changes nothing (`UNMATCHED`).
  - Removing Chase signs out every extra sign-in first.
  - A stopped sign-in no longer skips later ones.
  - "Every six months" is half-year.
  - "Earn $300 after you spend $3,000" is not progress.
- **Handbook**:
  - `overview.md` and `benefits-reader.md` describe the Chase path, sign-ins, coverage, the version 5 database and the privacy binding.
  - The map lists the Chase source files.
  - The receipt was renewed and verifies.
