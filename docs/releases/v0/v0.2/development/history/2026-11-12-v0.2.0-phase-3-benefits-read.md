## Subtasks

- T011: `sync/BenefitsSource.kt` (`BenefitsSource`, `BenefitsRead`, `BenefitsStop`) and `sync/webview/HeadlessWebViewBenefitsSource.kt`: a hidden WebView in the program's own profile opens the benefits entry page, receives the card list, reads the shown card, then reaches each other card by its last digits (ADR-0008). A fresh 128-bit nonce per page; messages accepted only through `BridgeProtocol.parse` with the page check; navigation outside the program's hosts blocked; a sign-in form stops the read (`SIGNED_OUT`); when the budget ends, cards read so far are returned and the rest reported as not read. Logs hosts, counts, and stop reasons only.
- T012: `SyncManager` starts the benefits read as its own job after a saved successful balance (refresh, `connect`, and the visible sign-in), never for `KEEP_ALIVE`, at most one per program, with its own fetch permit and budget (`BENEFITS_TIMEOUT_MS` 120 s, 30 s per page). `cancel`, `stop`, and `cancelHeadless` cancel it. `applyBenefits` writes the card list and each read card under the program lock; `clearCards` deletes cards at Sign out (`AppContainer.signOut`); Remove relies on the database cascade.
- T013: tests below.
