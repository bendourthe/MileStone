## Subtasks

- T017: `domain/BenefitPeriods.kt`: one month ("Nov", "November", "Sept") ends on that month's last day in the read date's year; a range ("Jul - Dec", "Oct - Dec", "Jan - Mar", "Nov - Jan", with "-", en dash, em dash, or "to") is the range containing or following the read date; yearly labels end on December 31; anything else gives no date. Length: monthly, quarterly, half-year, yearly. `SyncManager.applyBenefits` fills `periodEnd` and `periodLength` from the read date in the phone's time zone.
- T018: `sync/background/BenefitReminders.kt`: the rule (open credits with money left, a known period end within 7, 14, or 30 days by length, card read within 8 days, no record for the stable key and period end), recording before posting, pruning ended records; `AndroidReminderNotifier` with the "Benefit reminders" channel, `VISIBILITY_PRIVATE`, a public version "A card benefit expires soon", and a private text naming the card (no digits), the credit, the amount left, the period end, and the read date; `BenefitReminderScheduler` daily at 9:00 local through the existing `KeepAliveReceiver` (rescheduled at boot and update); a catch-up check in `AppContainer.refreshOnOpen`.
- T019: "Benefit reminders" switch in Settings (on by default, stored in its own private preferences file); a banner on the Benefits tab with an Allow button while reminders are on and notifications are not permitted; a reminder opens the app on its card page.

## Deviations

- `AndroidManifest.xml`, `data/settings/`, `MileStoneApplication.kt`, and `MainActivity.kt` are outside this plan's write paths. So the daily check reuses `KeepAliveReceiver` with its own action and request code; the setting lives in `PreferencesReminderSettings` (the app's backup rules already exclude all data); the channel is created in `AppContainer`; and `MileStoneApp` opens the card page from the reminder's launch intent (the intent clears the task, so the app starts fresh on the card).
- The first version ran a catch-up check when the app process started. Robolectric creates the application for every test class, so that check outlived its test and failed the next one; on the phone it would also run on alarm-only wake-ups. It now runs when the app is opened.
