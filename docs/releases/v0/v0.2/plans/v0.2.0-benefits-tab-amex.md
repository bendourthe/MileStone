# Plan -- Benefits Tab for Amex Cards

**Project**: MileStone
**Version**: v0.2.0
**Slug**: benefits-tab-amex
**Filename**: docs/releases/v0/v0.2/plans/v0.2.0-benefits-tab-amex.md
**Plan Type**: Feature
**Created**: 2026-11-12
**Goal**: A Benefits tab lists one tile per American Express card on the account, kept in step with the account on every Amex refresh except the 15-minute background keep-alive, and each card opens a page showing every benefit's progress as the Amex site reports it; when a statement credit's period is about to end with money unused, the phone shows one reminder; card details stay on the phone.

## Overview

MileStone v0.1.0 reads one balance per program. This plan adds a second kind of data for American Express: the cards on the account and each card's benefits (spend progress such as lounge-access thresholds, statement credits earned and left, and completed benefits). After an Amex balance read is saved, a separate benefits read loads the Amex benefits page in the same program profile, so it uses the session the balance read already has and never signs in by itself. It reads the card list and each card's benefits, updates the cards that were read, adds new cards, and removes a card only when a complete card list no longer shows it.

The app gains a third bottom tab, Benefits, between Rewards and Settings. Each card tile shows the card name and last digits, the next credit to expire with its amount left, and when its benefits were last read. A card page is grouped like the Amex site: spend progress, credits earned, completed, then anything else. A daily check posts one reminder per credit and period when the period ends soon and part of the credit is unused, with the lead time scaled to the period length.

Privacy follows v0.1.0: everything stays on the phone with no backup, logs name hosts and counts only, and the page script reads only named fields. A card is identified on the phone by its last digits; full card numbers, Amex account keys, and membership numbers (such as the Priority Pass number Amex shows) are never stored or logged, and grouped digit runs and long identifier-like tokens are stripped before any message leaves the page. Reminders show no card details on the lock screen.

## Definition of Done

1. On the owner's phone, after one Amex refresh, the Benefits tab shows one tile per card on the account, and every card's benefits match the Amex "Track Progress" page for that card (sections, earned and remaining amounts, periods), with no manual input.
2. A card added to or removed from the account appears or disappears after the next refresh that reads a complete card list.
3. A credit with money left near the end of its period produces one reminder, at the scaled lead time, and never twice for the same period; this is shown on a test clock in automated tests and observed on the phone when a real credit nears its period end.
4. Every check is green: the local gate, the instrumented suite on the emulator, and the integration pull request's required check.

## Scope

- **Must (the release can ship with these alone)**: Phases 1 to 4: storage, capture and extraction, the benefits read after an Amex refresh, and the Benefits tab with card pages.
- **Should**: Phase 5, reminders. If label parsing or notifications cannot be finished, v0.2.0 ships without them and the remainder is recorded as a known gap for v0.3, not a blocker.
- **Non-goals**: other issuers (added one at a time later); full card numbers or account keys on the phone; a configurable reminder lead time; reading benefits in the 15-minute keep-alive; enrolling in or activating benefits.

## Phase 1: Cards, benefits, and reminder storage

**Goal**: The app can store, update, and remove cards, their benefits, and sent-reminder records on the phone, with a migration that keeps every v0.1.0 account and balance.
**Prerequisites**: None.
**Stability Gate**: Room schema 3 is exported to `app/schemas/`, the 2-to-3 migration test passes on a v0.1.0 database, and the repository tests pass, including per-card replacement and cascades.
**Verification Expectation**: Run `.\gradlew.bat -q testDebugUnitTest --tests "*MigrationTest*" --tests "*Card*" --tests "*Benefit*"` and observe a v0.1.0 database opened at version 3 with its accounts and snapshots unchanged; a card renamed by a later read keeps its benefits and reminder records; removing the account deletes its cards, benefits, and reminder records; [[functional-verification]] owns the exercise record.
**Recommended model tier**: strong
**Recommended effort level**: high
**Rationale**: A schema migration on the owner's live data and new identity rules for cards and benefits carry data-loss and privacy risk, but the patterns already exist in v0.1.0.

### Sub-tasks

#### 1.1 -- Domain models and stable keys

**Objective**: Define the data and the identities the rest of the plan relies on.

**Failure modes**: A card without exactly 4 or 5 last digits is not stored and the read is marked incomplete; an amount that does not parse is stored as absent and the benefit still shows its title; two cards with the same last digits in one read mark the read incomplete instead of merging them.

**Prompt**:
> In `app/src/main/java/com/bendourthe/milestone/domain/`, add `Card` (programId, lastDigits of 4 or 5 digits, name as a mutable attribute normalized by trimming and removing trademark signs, sortOrder, cardListReadEpochMs, benefitsReadEpochMs), `BenefitSection` (SPEND_PROGRESS, CREDIT_EARNED, COMPLETED, OTHER), and `Benefit` (card key, stable key, section, position, title, earnedCents, remainingCents, targetCents, periodLabel, periodEnd, completed). The card key is programId plus lastDigits, so a renamed or upgraded card keeps its data. The benefit's stable key is the card key plus the normalized title plus the periodLabel, with position used only to tell apart two identical titles; section is not part of it, because a used credit moves to COMPLETED. Add `ReminderRecord` (benefit stable key, periodEnd, sentEpochMs). Never add a field for a full card number, an account key, or a membership number, and say so in the KDoc. Add `CardRepository` with `observeCards(programId)`, `observeBenefits(cardKey)`, `applyCardList(programId, cards, readEpochMs)` (upsert, and delete cards missing from the list only when the list is complete and non-empty), `applyCardBenefits(cardKey, benefits, readEpochMs)` (replace one card's benefits), and `deleteProgramCards(programId)`. Unit-test the keys, normalization, and duplicate detection.

## Phase 5: Benefit reminders (Should)

**Goal**: When a statement credit's period ends soon and part of it is unused, the phone shows one private reminder for that credit and period, at a lead time scaled to the period length.
**Prerequisites**: Phase 3 for the data; Phase 4 only for the card page a reminder opens.
**Stability Gate**: Period-end parsing and the reminder rule pass on a fixed clock, including a year boundary; one reminder per credit and period is posted and never repeated, including when credits reorder between reads; the lock-screen version shows no card details; the Settings toggle stops all reminders.
**Verification Expectation**: Run the reminder unit tests on a test clock and observe exactly one reminder for a monthly credit with money left 7 days before its period ends and for a half-year credit 30 days before, none for a used, completed, or spend-progress item, none a second time, and none when the card's benefits are older than 8 days; on the emulator, trigger the daily check with a seeded benefit and observe one notification in the "Benefit reminders" channel whose public version reads "A card benefit expires soon"; [[functional-verification]] owns the exercise record.
**Recommended model tier**: strong
**Recommended effort level**: high
**Rationale**: Date arithmetic from free-text period labels and a once-only notification rule are easy to get subtly wrong, but the scope is self-contained.
