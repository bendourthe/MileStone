# Plan - Benefits Category Grouping

**Project**: MileStone
**Version**: v0.7.0
**Filename**: v0.7.0-benefits-category-grouping.md
**Slug**: benefits-category-grouping
**Plan Type**: Feature
**Created**: 2026-11-17 (America/Los_Angeles)
**Status**: planned
**Goal**: Let users switch the Benefits overview between its existing Program > Card view and a Category > Card > Item view across all added institutions, preserving exact card identity, canonical benefits and merchant-offer presentation, local preferences, navigation state and issuer data, then verify and deliver the feature through green integration and the authorized release lifecycle.

## Overview

The owner confirmed collapsible categories followed by compact card headers and the existing full benefit tiles. The category view includes Welcome bonus, Credits, Spend trackers, Merchant offers and Other benefits. The existing institution/program view remains available. A visible two-option grouping control on the Benefits overview selects By program or By category; its last selected value is stored only on the device. Existing installations initially keep By program.

The category view reorganizes stored content. It does not discover cards, refresh issuer pages or enroll offers. Those capabilities remain owned by v0.5.0 and v0.6.0. Grouping must reuse their eventual integrated identities and presentation contracts rather than copy provisional structures from this older planning checkout. This request authorizes planning only, not implementation, publication or release.

## Goals First / Definition of Done

1. The Benefits overview provides labeled By program and By category choices below its header. The top-right Search action and existing header actions remain usable. The grouping choice survives app restart; missing or invalid stored values select By program without rewriting unrelated preferences.
2. Category mode includes stored items from every added supported institution and every verified card, including Chase Personal and Business coverage and linked connections. It never assumes Amex, four cards, particular card names, endings or capture counts. Same-card overlap is resolved only by the integrated identity owner.
3. Category sections appear in this order: Welcome bonus, Credits, Spend trackers, Merchant offers, Other benefits. Each contains compact card headers followed by existing item tiles. Card headers show institution/segment context, card artwork, name and masked ending. Their right-hand chevrons collapse that card within that category; tapping the header body opens the exact card through its canonical route.
4. Category and card collapse are independent. Other benefits and Merchant offers start collapsed on a new Benefits navigation entry; other categories start expanded. Every card group starts expanded, including cards inside initially collapsed categories. Newly arriving card groups use that same expanded default while existing choices are retained; arrivals never auto-expand a collapsed parent category. Collapse and scroll positions survive grouping switches, rotation and navigation away and Back for the current Benefits navigation entry. They do not overwrite the card-detail collapse preferences. Only the grouping mode must persist across process restart.
5. Reuse the canonical card-page projection, manual/issuer welcome precedence, classification, tracker/list duplicate merging, enrollment provenance, completed-credit progress and amount formatting. Reuse each card's saved sort and Completed last preference within each category. Card groups sort deterministically by program display name, segment, card display name and exact stable identity. Do not introduce a global item sort or combine monetary totals across cards/currencies.
6. Composite list keys include category, exact card identity, item kind and stable item identity. Two local welcome bonuses, equal benefit keys on different cards, equal endings on different cards and matching merchant offers on different eligible cards remain distinct. Category counts equal presented rows after canonical deduplication and the integrated offer visibility policy; collapsing does not change counts. Keep all category headers visible at zero and show a clear empty message; omit zero-item card groups.
7. Merchant offers reuse v0.6's separate offer model, visible/active counting policy, lifecycle labels and exact offer routes. They are not converted into benefit trackers or claimed Added from their presence in this view. Grouping, opening, scrolling, collapse and preference writes enqueue no issuer action. Default-on adding and its all-independently-eligible-card policy remain v0.6 responsibilities.
8. Full benefit tiles retain the approved inline Enrolled badge, completed styling, progress bar and current/target amount. Existing card alerts, acknowledgements, manual welcome editing and reminder actions remain reachable through the exact card page; this plan adds no bulk acknowledgement or second alert policy. Read failures, stale sections, removed cards and partial connection coverage are visible and never become fabricated empty or complete data.
9. Back from a card or offer returns to the prior grouping, expansion and scroll context. Search launched from category mode retains its established query/tab/Back behavior and still opens the canonical exact full destination. Refreshes/removals reconcile anchors by stable identity without substituting another card or item. Every header/toggle has a distinct accessible action, at least a 48 dp target, an expanded/collapsed announcement and a usable 200% font/narrow layout.
10. Observe the built flow with synthetic multi-institution data and genuine final phone acceptance. The complete local/full/platform/report and handbook gates, unchanged existing Search latency contract, required green integration, authorized release/archive/cleanup and native completion evidence must pass. Merely adding a selector, checking tasks or producing an APK is not delivery.
